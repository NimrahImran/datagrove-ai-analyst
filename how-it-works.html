How it works

From messy CSV to clear decisions

Five specialised AI agents work one after another. You can watch each one live while it works on your data.

01

Understanding agent

Learns what your data is about.

  • Detects delimiter and text encoding automatically
  • Labels every column as date, number, category, text or ID
  • Picks the main business metric, like Sales or Revenue
02

Cleaning agent

Makes your data trustworthy.

  • Removes empty rows and exact duplicates
  • Fixes messy text, like “lahore ” and “Lahore”
  • Fills missing values and flags extreme ones, without deleting real data
03

Analysis agent

Finds the story in your numbers.

  • Totals, averages and growth over time
  • Top and bottom groups, and how columns relate
  • A forecast of the next periods with a confidence band
04

Visualization agent

Turns findings into charts you can explore.

  • Chooses the right chart for each finding
  • Hover, filter, date range, zoom and drill-down
  • Recalculates instantly when you change filters
05

Report agent

Writes it all up for you.

  • Executive summary in simple words
  • Five insights and four practical recommendations
  • Downloadable PDF report and cleaned CSV
Under the hood

Built like a real product

A professional backend and a fast, interactive frontend.

Python + FastAPI

Agent pipeline with pandas and NumPy, streamed live to your browser.

Google Gemini

Writes insights, recommendations and chat answers from the real findings.

Plotly charts

Hover, zoom, range slider and drill-down, computed from your exact data.

Serverless on Vercel

Fast global hosting with no servers to manage.

Your data stays yours

Files are processed in memory and never stored. Nothing is shared or used for training. Close the page, and it is gone.

What kind of files can I upload?

Any CSV file up to 4 MB, such as sales, orders, marketing, finance or HR data. Larger files are sampled automatically so the analysis still works.

Is my data stored?

No. Your file is analyzed in memory and is not saved on our servers. Once you close the page, it is gone.

Do I need to know data science?

Not at all. The agents do the technical work and explain everything in simple words. You can also ask questions in the chat.

How accurate is the forecast?

It uses a proven trend method and shows a shaded confidence band, so you can see the likely range, not just one number.

Can I share the results?

Yes. Download the PDF report or the cleaned CSV and share it with your team or clients.
